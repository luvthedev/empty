# TaskFlow Prototype - Staging Access

## Overview

This is a **prototype** of the TaskFlow project management application, deployed to a staging environment for stakeholder review and feedback. This is not a production release.

| Field    | Value                              |

| URL      | *(provided separately by the team)* |

> **Note:** Credentials are shared privately. Contact the project team if you have not received them.

## How to Access

1. Open the staging URL in your web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge recommended).
2. When prompted, enter the **username** and **password** provided to you.
3. Browse through the prototype pages to review the design and user flow.

## What to Review

- **Home page** - Hero section, feature highlights, and overall layout
- **Project Boards** - Feature page showing board-style project management
- **Analytics** - Feature page showing metrics and project breakdowns

## Providing Feedback

When reviewing the prototype, please consider:

- Does the layout communicate the product value clearly?
- Are the feature descriptions accurate and compelling?
- Is the navigation intuitive?
- Any visual or content issues you notice?

Share your feedback with the project team via your preferred communication channel.

## Important Notes

- This is a **prototype for review purposes only** - it is not a functional application.
- The staging site is **password-protected** and not publicly accessible.
- Do not share the access credentials outside the stakeholder group.
- The site may be updated periodically; you will be notified of significant changes.
- If the site is unreachable, contact the project team for assistance.
