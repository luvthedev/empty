# Staging Access Instructions

## Overview

The TaskFlow prototype is available on a password-protected staging site for stakeholder review. This is a prototype intended for collecting feedback — it is not a production environment.

> Credentials will be shared via a secure channel. Do not share them publicly.

## How to Access

1. Open the staging URL in your browser.
2. You will be prompted for a username and password.
3. Enter the credentials provided to you.
4. Browse the prototype — all pages are accessible from the navigation.

## What to Review

- **Home page** — hero section, product overview, and statistics.
- **Feature pages** — detailed descriptions of core functionality.
- **Navigation** — links between pages and overall user flow.
- **Visual design** — layout, typography, and responsiveness.

## Providing Feedback

Please note any feedback on:
- Missing content or unclear messaging
- Navigation issues or broken links
- Visual or layout problems on your device
- Any features you expected to see but are missing

## Important Notes

- This is a **prototype** — not all features are functional.
- The site is **password-protected** and not publicly accessible.
- Content and design may change based on feedback.
- If you experience access issues, contact the development team.
